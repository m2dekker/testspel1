Blackwake Isles

Sailing test. Find islands, scout them, and take the ones worth having.

  • The wind decides your speed. Sailing across the wind is fastest. Close to the wind you crawl, and straight into it you stop. Watch the wind arrow.
  • Islands appear on your map as you sail past them.
  • Get closer with your spyglass to see who holds an island. Raider and Navy forts open fire once you're inside their range (the red dashed circle), so you have time to turn back.
  • Silence a fort with broadsides, then slow down and land your crew. They lay siege, and the island becomes yours when the siege timer ends. You can sail on in the meantime.
  • You can hold 3 islands besides your haven. Raiders try to take them back, so build forts on them from the world map. Higher fort levels need iron.
  • Islands you hold produce their resource, also while you're away. Your home island repairs your hull.

This test starts at Dev pace (nearly instant timers). Switch to Live pace on the world map to feel the real timings.

Phone: drag on the left half to steer, buttons on the right fire and set sail. Keyboard: A/D steer, W/S sails, Q/E fire, M map.
