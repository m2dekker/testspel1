Blackwake Isles

Sailing test. Find islands, scout them, and take the ones worth having.

  • Islands appear on your map as you sail past them.
  • Get closer with your spyglass to see who holds an island. Raider and Navy forts open fire once you're inside their range (the red dashed circle), so you have time to turn back.
  • Silence a fort with broadsides, then slow down near the island and land your crew to capture it. Unclaimed islands can be taken right away.
  • Every island you hold produces its resource for you, also while you're away. Your home island repairs your hull.

Phone: drag on the left half to steer, buttons on the right fire and set sail. Keyboard: A/D steer, W/S sails, Q/E fire, M map.
