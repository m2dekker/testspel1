# Blackwake Isles – Complete Design & MVP Document
**Single source of truth for Claude**

## 1. Project Overview

**Working Title:** Blackwake Isles

**Vision**
Mobile-first real-time multiplayer strategy game inspired by Ikariam, re-themed as an Age of Sail / Caribbean pirate world. Players build pirate havens on islands, manage resources, trade, raid, expand to new islands, and form Brotherhoods (alliances).

**Core Pillars**
1. Phone-first (portrait, one-handed, 2–5 minute meaningful sessions)
2. Island specialization forces trade and interaction
3. Naval focus (trade + combat)
4. Fair real-time progression (feels good offline)
5. Fast path to a fun MVP

**Target Platforms**
- Primary: iOS + Android (Flutter)
- Secondary: Web (same Flutter codebase)

**Success Criteria for MVP**
- New player can found a second colony and trade within the first session
- Two players can trade or raid each other
- Feels good on mid-range phones
- Core loop is addictive enough for day-2 retention

- Account creation / login (email + Google + Apple)
- One starting island with Timber + 1 random luxury resource
- Haven building system (place + upgrade buildings)
- Citizen/Crew assignment (production vs idle/gold)

- Basic research system (simplified 4 trees)

- Deep espionage

- Advanced diplomacy

## 3. Core Game Systems

### Haven / City System
- Players start with one small pirate haven.
- Buildings are placed on limited plots.
- Most buildings can be upgraded (levels 1–10 is enough for MVP).
- Crew (citizens) are assigned to production buildings or left idle (generate gold).

### Economy & Trade
- Every island produces Timber + one luxury resource.
- Players must trade or raid to obtain the other luxuries.
- Simple market: players can post buy/sell orders.
- Cargo ships have capacity and travel time based on distance.
- Gold comes from idle crew + successful trade.

### Research (4 simplified trees)
- **Seafaring**: ship speed, capacity, new ship types, colonization
- **Economy**: production bonuses, storage, market fees
- **Warfare**: ship combat strength, defenses, unit unlocks
- **Intrigue**: faster colonization, better scouting, basic treaties

Research points are generated by the Academy + assigned scholars.

### Expansion / Colonization
- Research "Chart New Waters".
- Build a Colony Ship.
- Choose an empty slot on another island.
- New colony starts small and requires a Pirate Stronghold.

### Military (Lean)
- Naval combat is the main focus.
- Simple auto-resolver with light tactical influence.
- Ships can trade, raid, or blockade.
- Soft shield protects new and recently attacked players.

### Social – Brotherhoods
- Create or join a Brotherhood.
- Shared chat.
- Members can see limited information about each other.

### Offline Progression
- Production, research, and construction continue while offline.
- Soft protection (8–12 hours) after login or after being attacked.
- Push notifications for important events.

## 4. Art Direction

**Style:** Stylized, colorful, slightly cartoony, high readability.
Think a cleaner, mobile-friendly mix of Sea of Thieves energy + classic pirate charm.

**Visual Pillars**
- Bright turquoise water
- Sandy beaches and lush green islands
- Warm wooden architecture with pirate details (flags, lanterns, cannons, ropes)
- Clear silhouettes even at small sizes
- Cheerful and adventurous tone (charming rogues)

**Color Palette**
- Ocean: vibrant turquoise + deep blue
- Land: warm greens, sandy beige, rocky greys
- UI: dark wood, parchment, gold accents, high contrast

**MVP Art Scope**
- Haven view (isometric or elevated 2.5D)
- 10 building sprites (different levels can be simple variations)
- 3 ship types
- Basic unit/crew icons
- Island overview map
- Simple captain avatar system
- UI kit (buttons, panels, resource icons)

**Animation Level for MVP:** Very light (idle animations, simple ship movement, floating resource numbers).

## 5. Monetization (Fair)

**Model:** Free-to-play with cosmetic + convenience focus.

**MVP Monetization**
- Cosmetics: ship skins, flags, haven decorations, captain outfits
- Convenience: extra build queue, temporary protection boosts, small speed-ups (capped)
- Soft currency (Doubloons) from gameplay
- Hard currency (Pieces of Eight) mainly for cosmetics and light convenience

**Avoid in MVP:** Pay-to-win strength, large resource packs that create big power gaps, aggressive energy systems.

## 8. Direction Update (25 Oct 2026): Sail and Capture

**Why:** the haven-only loop (tap, wait) is not fun on its own. Players want to *sail*.
The game becomes two halves that feed each other:

- **At sea (action):** steer your own ship in real time across a procedurally generated
  archipelago, scout islands, fight forts, and capture islands.
- **At home (strategy):** the haven economy from Sprints 1–2. Captured islands supply
  resources; the haven turns them into better ships, crew and research.

### Core sea loop
1. Sail out. Islands appear on the world map as you pass them (fog of war).
2. Get close and use the spyglass to see who holds an island: **Unclaimed**, an NPC
   faction (**Black Tide Raiders**, **Crown Navy**) or, later, another player.
3. The owner is revealed **before** you are inside their fort's cannon range, so you
   always get the chance to retreat.
4. Silence the fort with broadsides, slow down, and land your crew to capture it.
5. Captured islands produce their resource for you, also while you are offline.

### World generation
- Every world is generated from a **seed**. The same seed always gives the same islands
  (positions, shapes, names, owners, resources, fort strength).
- Multiple worlds later = multiple seeds (one row per world in a `worlds` table).
- Safer waters near the home island; stronger factions further out.

### Prototype
`prototypes/sailing/index.html` is a playable single-file test of this loop (plain JS
canvas, open it in a browser). It proves the feel before it is rebuilt in the app.

### Open questions for the real build
- **Engine:** the sea view needs a game loop. Plan: Flame (Flutter's 2D game engine)
  inside the existing app, so the haven screens stay as they are.
- **World data:** generate each world once on the server from its seed and store the
  islands, rather than generating on every client.
- **Cheating in real-time fights:** the server cannot simulate every cannonball. MVP:
  fights against NPC forts are simulated on the device, and the server checks that a
  capture is plausible (ship was near, enough time passed, fort strength vs. ship).
- **Player vs player:** islands held by other players are defended by their fort while
  they are offline (asynchronous). Live ship-to-ship PvP comes later, if at all.
